Six things were broken. Taking the pictures is how I found them.

Each one passed every server-side check while the screen was wrong, which is the whole argument for photographing rather than asserting.

Every service drew the same sparkle

A photographer, a balloon arch and a cake were one identical glyph on the matching board.

The board's query never selected service_id or trade, so the sprite fell through to its default on every row. The data was right and the screen could not say what it was about — this is the screen you called dull. Now camera, balloon, cake.

The home card merged every booking into one

A three-service booking read “+9”, under one booking's date, linking wherever the sort landed.

It was reading every open line the customer had, across every request. One card is now one booking, and the others are named underneath rather than silently folded in.

Decor could not be specified at all

A photo corner and a fully decorated hall were the same button at the same price.

It now asks how much of the space and balloons or flowers. The first cut of this was worse than the gap: it multiplied a tier the system had already picked from headcount, quoting ₹9,594 for a job the rate card prices at ₹30,360. The question now selects the real rate-card row, and a script checks the screen and the server against each other on all 45 combinations.

A partner's dead service row said nothing

A real partner typed “videpgraphy”. Dispatch matches on the trade, so that row has never been offered to them once.

The row now says it is never offered, and only that row. The form requires a trade from a list, so no new row can be born dead — this is what was making a newly-signed partner invisible.

The options card was headed “decor”

Lower-case, the raw id, beside “Photography” and “Celebration cake”.

Decor and catering are deliberately absent from the pricing catalogue — they have their own rate cards — so the name lookup fell through to the id. It now uses the trade name.

A guard script was crying wolf

Two “undefined components that throw at render” were component names written in prose inside doc comments.

It read comments as code. It now strips them — and I broke a real component to confirm it still catches the thing it is for. A check that reports things that are fine is a check people learn to run past, and this one was sitting in front of a deploy decision.

Checks that can fail, and did

Every one of these exits non-zero on a real fault — I sabotaged the new ones to prove it rather than trusting a green run.

That has to come off before real customers arrive, and it is a decision rather than a bug.

What the pictures show

What these pictures do not prove

Naming the gaps, because a gap you have to notice is a gap that gets missed.

  • The paid confirmation and the date-blocked card. Razorpay's checkout is a hosted iframe on their domain — it cannot be driven headlessly, and a faked screenshot of a payment screen would be a lie about the one thing that must not be lied about. This needs your ₹1 test payment, and I have the steps ready.
  • Push arriving on a physical phone. The alerts path is fixed for the WebView case and every failure reason now has a sentence a person can act on, but a notification landing on your handset is not something this machine can witness.
  • The APK. These are captures of the exact bundle the APK wraps, served locally, not of the installed app. Same code, different shell.
  • Every button. I drove the flows and exercised the controls that carry money or dispatch — accept, pay, cancel, the trade picker, the menus, the tabs. I did not click all several hundred controls in the app, and I am not going to claim I did.

Before this goes to production

Three things, and two of them are yours to decide.

The client and the server must ship together

Decor pricing changed on both sides. The deployed API still carries the old bundle, so until this deploy lands the screen and the server would disagree on a decor booking. Nothing half-deploys here.

The 221 seeded partners

They are reachable from your account in production today, by design, so you could test. Before a real customer books, either remove your account from SYNTHETIC_DEMO_CUSTOMER or purge them. Say which and I will do it.
